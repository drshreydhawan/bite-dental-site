Our origin

Why we built Bite Dental.

Dr. Shrey Dhawan completed his MDS in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics and kept running into the same problem: patients who needed specialist-level care couldn't get it without long hospital waits or corporate-clinic prices.

So, with Dr. Shamindhi Dhawan, he co-founded Bite Dental. Founded in Meera Bagh, the clinic was built around a simple rule: every patient gets the assessment and treatment a senior specialist would give a private referral. No generalist-led work on cases that need a specialist. No upselling. The quote you get at consultation is the quote you pay.

Today we care for patients at two clinics, Meera Bagh (West Delhi) and MedHarbour Hospital, Sector 51 (Gurugram), with each core discipline led by its own specialist.

How we practise

The Bite philosophy.

Four principles behind every decision, from greeting a nervous six-year-old to planning a full-arch implant case.

Specialist-led, always

Implants by our implant surgeons, aligners and braces by our Invisalign Platinum Elite orthodontist, children by our paediatric dentist. Generalists don't lead specialist procedures here.

Evidence-based

Protocols follow current clinical evidence and IDA guidelines. We recommend treatments on outcome data, not trends.

No surprises on price

The quote at consultation is what you pay. If a clinical finding changes the plan, we explain it and agree it with you first. 0% EMI available.

Fear-free dentistry

Tell-Show-Do for every anxious patient, a stop-any-time signal, and appointments that are never rushed.

Credentials

Verifiable qualifications. Not claims.

MDS specialists

Our specialists hold an MDS, India's postgraduate dental qualification: three years of full-time specialist training beyond BDS.

Invisalign® Platinum Elite

Premium implant systems

Leading aligner brands
