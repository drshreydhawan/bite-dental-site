Our Origin

Why we built Bite Dental.

Dr. Shrey Dhawan completed his MDS in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics from a leading Indian dental institution and went on to become one of a rare few dentists in India to earn Invisalign Platinum Elite status — placing him in the top 5% of providers globally. During his postgraduate training, he kept encountering the same frustration: patients who genuinely needed specialist-level care, but couldn't access it without visiting a government hospital with long waits or paying corporate clinic prices they couldn't afford.

Bite Dental was the answer to that gap. Founded in West Delhi's Meera Bagh, the clinic was built from day one around a simple operating principle: every patient who walks in gets the same quality of assessment and treatment that a senior specialist would give a private referral. No GP-led work on cases that need a specialist. No upselling. A quote given at consultation is the quote you pay.

Today, Bite Dental operates across two clinics — Meera Bagh (West Delhi) and Med Harbour, Sector 31 (Gurugram) — with a core team of three MDS specialists, each leading their respective discipline. Over 1500 patients have been treated. The 4.9-star Google rating reflects not a marketing campaign, but a consistent clinical standard across every single visit.

How We Practice

The Bite Philosophy.

Four principles that determine every clinical decision — from how we greet a nervous six-year-old to how we plan a full-arch implant case.

🏅
Specialist-Led, Always
Clear aligners are planned by Dr. Shrey Dhawan — Invisalign Platinum Elite. Implants are placed by Dr. Shamindhi Dhawan — 5000+ cases. Kids are seen by Dr. Achla Goel — MDS Pedodontics. GP dentists do not lead specialist procedures at Bite Dental. This is not how most clinics operate. It's how we do.
📋
Evidence-Based Treatment
Every protocol at Bite Dental follows current clinical evidence — aligned with IDA guidelines, Cochrane dental reviews, and manufacturer clinical data for the implant and aligner systems we use. We don't offer treatments based on trend; we offer them based on outcome data.
💬
Transparent Pricing, No Surprises
The quote you receive at consultation is the price you pay. No hidden charges. No add-ons that appear midway through treatment. If the scope changes due to a clinical finding, we tell you first and get your agreement — nothing proceeds without your informed consent and full cost understanding. 0% EMI available.
🛡️
Fear-Free Dentistry
We use the Tell-Show-Do method across all treatments — especially for children and anxious patients. Every patient has a stop-at-any-time signal. We don't rush appointments. Dental anxiety is real, and our job is to remove it step by step, not dismiss it. The goal is a patient who comes back voluntarily — not one who has to be coaxed.
