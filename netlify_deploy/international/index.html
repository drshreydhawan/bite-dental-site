3D iTero Scanner

No goopy impressions. The iTero intraoral scanner maps your teeth in 3D in minutes — used for aligner treatment, crowns, and smile design previews before any procedure.

Planning Your Visit

How it works

Most international patients combine dental treatment with a trip to Delhi. We make it simple — from virtual consultation to final appointment.

1
Send an Enquiry

WhatsApp or email us with your X-rays, photos, or just a description. We'll respond within 24 hours with an initial treatment plan and quote.

2
Virtual Consult

A free 20-minute video call with Dr. Shrey to review your case, answer questions, and confirm the treatment plan and timeline before you book flights.

3
Plan Your Trip

We'll give you a day-by-day appointment schedule so you know exactly how many days to plan for. Most multi-treatment visits are 5–10 days. Delhi has direct flights from most hubs.

4
Treatment & Follow-up

Treatment with your specialist. We provide full documentation for your home dentist. WhatsApp follow-up support for 90 days after you leave.

🛂
Visa & Entry

Most nationalities can obtain an India e-Tourist Visa online within 72 hours at indianvisaonline.gov.in. The 30-day single entry e-Visa is sufficient for most treatment trips. We can provide a treatment letter if your visa office requests it. Delhi is served by Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) with direct flights from London, Dubai, Singapore, New York, and most major hubs.
