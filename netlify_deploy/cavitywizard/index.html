The science, simply

Your saliva tells the whole story.
We just know how to read it.

Long before a cavity appears, cavity-causing bacteria (S. mutans and Lactobacillus) quietly multiply in your child's saliva. Their levels — combined with how acidic the saliva is and how well it neutralises acid — are proven, measurable predictors of decay. Think of it as a credit score for your child's teeth.

At the clinic

The whole thing takes 8 minutes.
No needles. No drama.

🧊
Chew & Collect
Your child chews a small piece of paraffin wax for 5 minutes. It tastes like nothing. The chewing stimulates saliva — that's the entire sample collection. No needles, no swabs in the throat, no discomfort.
5 minutes
🔬
Lab Does the Work
The dentist runs 4 quick chairside tests on the spot (pH strips, saliva volume). The sample then goes to our NABL-certified lab partner where bacterial cultures are grown and counted over 48 hours.
