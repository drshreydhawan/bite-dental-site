Dental implant prices in Delhi-NCR can differ by a factor of two or three from one clinic to the next, and that's not because some clinics are "overcharging". Implants are not one product. This guide explains what is inside the price, so you can compare quotes fairly.

## What is included in an implant price?

A single-tooth implant is made of three parts, and a complete quote should cover all of them:

- **The implant (titanium post)** placed in the jawbone.
- **The abutment**, the connector that holds the tooth.
- **The crown**, the visible tooth on top.

Some clinics advertise only the post and add the abutment and crown later. Always ask: "Is this the **total cost for one finished tooth**?"

## What does a single implant cost at Bite Dental?

Single-tooth implants at Bite Dental start from **₹35,000, going up to about ₹55,000** depending on the implant brand and how complex your case is. That figure is for the finished tooth. Multiple-tooth and full-arch treatments are quoted after a scan and examination.

## Why are some implants much cheaper?

Some clinics advertise implants at prices well below this. Usually the difference is in:

1. **The implant brand.** Well-documented international systems cost more than unbranded or lesser-known ones.
2. **The crown material.** Zirconia, metal-ceramic and basic crowns differ in price and look.
3. **The planning.** Scans and guided planning cost money, and they matter for placing the implant exactly where it should go.
4. **The surgeon.** Experience and specialist training affect outcomes, particularly in difficult cases.

Cheaper is not automatically worse, but you should know exactly what you're being offered.

## Extra costs to ask about

Not every patient needs these, but they change the final bill:

- **Bone grafting**, when there isn't enough bone to hold the implant.
- **Sinus lift**, for some upper back teeth.
- **A 3D scan (CBCT)** to plan placement.
- **Extraction of the old tooth**, if it's still there.

## How long does the whole process take?

It depends on your bone and whether the implant can be placed straight after an extraction. In many cases the implant needs a few months to fuse with the bone before the final crown is fitted. Your surgeon will give you a timeline after the scan.

## Implant, bridge or dentures?

An implant replaces the root as well as the tooth, and it doesn't involve grinding down healthy neighbouring teeth the way a bridge does. A bridge is usually quicker and costs less upfront. The right choice depends on your bone, your gums, your other teeth and your budget, and a good dentist will explain all three.

## How to compare two implant quotes

Ask each clinic to write down:

- The implant brand and country of manufacture
- Whether the abutment and crown are included, and the crown material
- Whether scans and extractions are included
- Who will place the implant, and their qualifications
- What follow-up and warranty are offered
