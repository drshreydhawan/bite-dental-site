If you have a toothache and you've searched for "root canal cost in Delhi", you've probably seen prices ranging from a few thousand rupees to well over ten thousand. That range is confusing, and it makes it hard to tell whether a quote is fair. This guide explains what drives the price, what a good quote includes, and the questions worth asking before you commit.

## Short answer: what does a root canal cost?

The cost depends mostly on **which tooth** is being treated, because back teeth have more canals and take longer to clean and seal.

| Tooth | Typical canals | What it means for the procedure |
|---|---|---|
| Front tooth (incisor, canine) | 1 | Usually the simplest and quickest |
| Premolar | 1–2 | Mid-complexity |
| Molar (back tooth) | 3–4 | Most time-consuming and technically demanding |

## What changes the price of a root canal?

1. **The tooth and number of canals.** Molars cost more than front teeth.
2. **How complicated the anatomy is.** Curved or very narrow canals take longer.
3. **Whether it's a first treatment or a retreatment.** Redoing an old root canal is more complex and costs more.
4. **Who does the treatment.** A specialist endodontist (MDS) works with magnification and specialist instruments, which can be the difference between saving a tooth and losing it.
5. **Whether the crown is included.**

## Don't forget the crown

A root canal treats the inside of the tooth. Back teeth usually need a **crown** afterwards to protect them from cracking, and the crown is normally billed separately. When you compare two clinics, always ask whether the quote includes:

- The X-ray (and any 3D scan if needed)
- The medicines and anaesthetic
- Any temporary filling
- The final crown, and which material it is made from

A low headline price that leaves out the crown is not actually cheaper.

## Is a root canal painful?

With modern anaesthesia, the procedure itself should feel similar to having a filling done. The pain most people remember comes from the infection **before** treatment, not from the treatment itself. Some soreness for a day or two afterwards is normal and is usually managed with simple painkillers.

## One visit or more?

Many straightforward cases can be completed in a single visit. Some teeth need more than one appointment, for example if there is a lot of infection or swelling that must settle first. Your dentist can tell you after the X-ray.

## Root canal or extraction?

Saving your natural tooth is usually the better long-term option when it can be saved. Pulling the tooth leaves a gap that later needs an implant or a bridge, and that usually costs more than a root canal and crown together. Your dentist will tell you honestly if a tooth cannot be saved.

## Questions to ask any clinic before you agree

- Is the dentist a specialist in root canal treatment (an MDS Endodontist)?
- What exactly is included in this price?
- Will I need a crown afterwards, and what will it cost?
- How many visits do you expect?
- What happens if the tooth needs retreatment later?
