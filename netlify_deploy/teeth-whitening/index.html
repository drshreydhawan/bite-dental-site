Why Professional Whitening?

Strips and charcoal can't do this.

There's a significant difference between what you buy at a chemist and what we use in the clinic. Here's why it matters.

Feature OTC Strips / Charcoal / Whitening Toothpaste Professional Whitening at Bite Dental
Peroxide concentration Up to 6% (legal OTC limit in India) 35% prescription-grade hydrogen peroxide
Tray fit Generic — gel leaks onto gums Custom-fitted trays — precise, no gum contact
Sensitivity management No assessment, no support Pre-treatment assessment + desensitising gel
Gum protection None — can cause gum irritation Gum barrier applied before every session
Expected result 1–2 shades over several weeks Up to 8 shades — in 60 minutes
Longevity 2–4 weeks typically 12–24 months with maintenance
Charcoal toothpaste Abrasive — erodes enamel over time, no bleaching action Not used — we protect enamel, not scratch it
A note on charcoal toothpaste: It doesn't bleach teeth — it abrades them. The perceived whitening effect comes from scratching surface stains off enamel, which simultaneously thins it. Thinner enamel makes the yellower dentine beneath more visible over time. We see the results of prolonged charcoal use regularly in our clinic. Please stop.

Is It Right For You?

Who benefits — and who to pause.

Whitening works brilliantly for most people. But there are a few situations where we'll be upfront and tell you to wait or consider alternatives.

✅ Great candidates for whitening

Is It Safe?

The honest answer on enamel safety.

There's a lot of confusion between bleaching and enamel erosion. They're completely different processes — and professional whitening, done correctly, causes neither enamel loss nor permanent damage.

Bleaching works by breaking down organic colour molecules (chromogens) within the enamel structure using hydrogen peroxide. It does not remove enamel. Erosion is caused by acid — from fizzy drinks, citrus, charcoal pastes — which chemically dissolves enamel over time.

Sensitivity is the most common side effect — it's temporary. The hydrogen peroxide temporarily makes enamel more porous, which can make teeth more reactive for 24–48 hours. We use potassium nitrate-based desensitising gel before and after every whitening session to minimise this.

Myths vs Facts
Myth Whitening destroys enamel.
Fact Professional hydrogen peroxide bleaching does not remove enamel. It oxidises colour molecules. Studies confirm no measurable enamel loss at clinical concentrations.
Myth Sensitivity means it's damaging your teeth.
Fact Temporary sensitivity is a physiological response to peroxide — not a sign of damage. It resolves within 24–48 hours. We manage it proactively with desensitising treatment.
Myth Charcoal is a natural, safe alternative.
Fact Charcoal is abrasive. It scratches surface stains off by physically grinding enamel. Over time, this thins enamel and makes teeth look more yellow, not less.

The Process

What happens at your appointment.

1
Consultation & Shade Mapping
We photograph your teeth and map your current shade on the Vita guide. We discuss your target shade, check for contraindications (crowns in smile zone, gum disease), and confirm you're a suitable candidate. All this is free.
2
Gum Protection
A liquid dental dam is applied to your gum tissue and set with a curing light — protecting your gums entirely from the whitening gel. This is what separates professional whitening from OTC strips that can cause gum burns.
3
Gel Application
Professional-grade hydrogen peroxide whitening gel is applied evenly across all visible teeth. The Zoom LED light is then positioned over your mouth — its blue light accelerates the peroxide's oxidation reaction.
4
Light Activation (3 × 15 min rounds)
The Zoom light activates the gel in three 15-minute cycles, with fresh gel applied between each round. You simply relax in the chair — many patients watch something on their phone. Total active time: approximately 45 minutes.
5
Desensitising Gel + Reveal
After the final round, we apply a potassium nitrate desensitising gel for 5 minutes to reduce post-treatment sensitivity. Then the retractor comes out, we rinse, and you see your result — the full shade change, right then and there.
1
Impressions & Shade Assessment
We take accurate impressions of your upper and lower teeth. These are used to fabricate custom whitening trays that fit your exact dental arch — no gaps, no gel leakage. Your baseline shade is mapped and photographed for comparison later.
2
Custom Trays Fabricated
Your custom trays are ready within 2–3 working days. We call you in for a fitting to make sure they seat perfectly and show you exactly how much gel to use per tray. Using too much gel is a common mistake with OTC kits — we eliminate that.
3
Gel Kit & Instructions
You take home your custom trays and a full course of prescription-strength whitening gel — syringes with enough gel for your complete 2-week protocol. We give you written and verbal instructions, including what to avoid and when to stop if sensitivity occurs.
4
2-Week Home Protocol
Wear your trays for 30–60 minutes daily (or as instructed based on your gel concentration). The gradual approach is gentler on sensitivity while building up to the full whitening result. Most patients see a noticeable difference by day 5–7.
5
Review & Maintenance Plan
We review your results at the end of the protocol (in-clinic or via photo). Your custom trays are yours to keep — for annual top-ups, simply order a fresh gel course. We apply a fluoride varnish to remineralise enamel after your whitening course.
Aftercare

Protect your investment.

The first 48 hours post-whitening are the most critical. Follow these guidelines and your results will last significantly longer.

🚫 The Whitening Diet (48 hrs post-treatment)

Avoid anything that would stain a white shirt:
☕Coffee & tea — even with milk
🍷Red wine & dark-coloured drinks
🫐Berries — blueberries, pomegranate, cherries
🌿Turmeric, saffron, beetroot
🍅Tomato sauce, soy sauce, balsamic
🚬Tobacco — smoking or chewing
Stick to: water, milk, white rice, chicken, fish, white pasta, banana, bread — the "white diet."

✅ Long-Term Maintenance Tips

💧Rinse with water immediately after consuming staining drinks
🥤Use a straw for coffee, tea, and cold drinks to minimise tooth contact
🦷Use a whitening toothpaste (not abrasive charcoal) for maintenance
🔬Professional clean every 6 months to remove surface staining before it sets
⚡Annual top-up session (in-chair or home kit) keeps results looking fresh
🛡️Fluoride varnish post-whitening to remineralise enamel — we apply this at your visit
🚭Stop tobacco — the single biggest thing you can do for lasting whiteness
